### Q1: What are the main date palm varieties grown in Yemen's Hadhramaut Valley and how many local cultivars exist?

**EN:** Hadhramaut Valley is the heartland of Yemeni date palm cultivation, home to over 40 documented local cultivars adapted to the wadi's hot, arid microclimate. The diversity is exceptional for such a confined growing area. Key varieties include:

| Variety (EN) | الصنف (AR) | Characteristics | Primary Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Barni | برني | Large, amber-brown, semi-dry | Fresh & packed dates |
| Madeeni | مديني | Medium, dark, soft | Rutab (fresh soft) consumption |
| Khasab | خصب | Small-medium, high sugar | Date syrup (dibs) |
| Baghal | بغل | Very large fruit, thick flesh | Premium table dates |
| Ajeeb | عجيب | Oval, sweet, early ripening | Fresh eating |
| Sukari Hadhrami | سكري حضرمي | Amber, crisp at Khalal stage | Fresh & storage |
| Um al-Khashab | أم الخشب | Hard, long shelf life | Storage & trade |
| Sayer Hadhrami | صاير حضرمي | Dark, soft, high yield | Processing & syrup |
| Gash | قش | Light-colored, dry type | Long-term storage |
| Beid al-Hamama | بيض الحمامة | White-yellow, delicate flavor | Fresh premium market |

Other cultivars include Qarn, Mabroom, Rasheediya, Tawahi, and dozens of local landraces maintained by individual farming families across the Wadi Hadhramaut oases from Tarim to Shibam to Seiyun.

### Q2: Why is Hadhramaut considered the center of Yemeni date production and what is its share of national output?

**EN:** Hadhramaut governorate produces approximately 67% of Yemen's total date output, making it the undisputed center of Yemeni date palm cultivation. Several factors explain this dominance:

| Factor | Details |
|---|---|
| **Geography** | Wadi Hadhramaut is a deep, sheltered valley (600-700m elevation) with alluvial soils ideal for date palms |
| **Climate** | Hot, dry summers (40-48 C) with very low humidity -- perfect for fruit ripening and drying |
| **Water** | Spate irrigation from seasonal floods (sayl) and shallow wells along the wadi |
| **Heritage** | Over 2,000 years of continuous date cultivation; traditional knowledge passed through generations |
| **Tree Count** | Estimated 2.5-3 million date palms in Hadhramaut alone |
| **Cultural Identity** | Dates are central to Hadhrami food culture, trade, and social customs |

Yemen's total date production is estimated at 50,000-65,000 tonnes annually. Hadhramaut contributes roughly 33,500-43,500 tonnes. The main production centers within Hadhramaut are:

- **Wadi Hadhramaut** (Tarim, Seiyun, Shibam, Qatn): the primary belt
- **Wadi Daw'an**: secondary production area
- **Wadi Amd**: smaller but significant orchards
- **Coastal Mukalla**: limited coastal production

The remainder of Yemen's date production comes from Marib (eastern plateau), Shabwa, Tihama coastal areas, and the southern coast (Aden, Lahj, Abyan).

**AR:** تنتج محافظة حضرموت حوالي 67% من إجمالي إنتاج اليمن من التمور، مما يجعلها المركز الرئيسي بلا منازع لزراعة النخيل اليمني. تفسر عدة عوامل هذه الهيمنة:

| العامل | التفاصيل |
|---|---|
| **الجغرافيا** | وادي حضرموت واد عميق محمي (600-700م ارتفاع) بتربة طميية مثالية لنخيل التمر |
| **المناخ** | صيف حار وجاف (40-48°م) مع رطوبة منخفضة جداً -- مثالي لنضج الثمار وتجفيفها |
| **المياه** | ري سيلي من الفيضانات الموسمية (السيل) وآبار ضحلة على طول الوادي |
| **التراث** | أكثر من 2000 سنة من زراعة النخيل المستمرة؛ معرفة تقليدية متوارثة عبر الأجيال |
| **عدد الأشجار** | يُقدر بنحو 2.5-3 مليون نخلة في حضرموت وحدها |
| **الهوية الثقافية** | التمور محورية في ثقافة الغذاء الحضرمي والتجارة والعادات الاجتماعية |

يُقدر إجمالي إنتاج اليمن من التمور بـ 50,000-65,000 طن سنوياً. تساهم حضرموت بنحو 33,500-43,500 طن. مراكز الإنتاج الرئيسية داخل حضرموت هي:

- **وادي حضرموت** (تريم، سيئون، شبام، القطن): الحزام الإنتاجي الرئيسي
- **وادي دوعن**: منطقة إنتاج ثانوية
- **وادي عمد**: بساتين أصغر لكنها مهمة
- **المكلا الساحلية**: إنتاج ساحلي محدود

يأتي ما تبقى من إنتاج اليمن من التمور من مأرب (الهضبة الشرقية)، شبوة، مناطق تهامة الساحلية، والساحل الجنوبي (عدن، لحج، أبين).

### Q3: How is date palm cultivation practiced in Najran (southern Saudi Arabia) and what role does the Najran Dam play?

**EN:** Najran, located in the southwestern corner of Saudi Arabia near the Yemeni border, is one of the Kingdom's important date-producing regions with an estimated 2+ million date palms. The region benefits from a semi-arid highland climate (1,200-1,500m elevation) and the vital infrastructure of the Najran Dam (Wadi Najran Dam).

**Najran Dam's Role:**

| Aspect | Details |
|---|---|
| **Capacity** | ~86 million cubic meters |
| **Purpose** | Flood control, groundwater recharge, agricultural irrigation |
| **Impact on Agriculture** | Stabilized water table, enabled expansion of date orchards along Wadi Najran |
| **Irrigation Method** | Primarily supports groundwater wells; farmers pump from recharged aquifer |
| **Coverage** | Supports approximately 8,000+ hectares of agricultural land |

**Najran Date Varieties:**

| Variety | الصنف | Characteristics |
|---|---|---|
| Najrani (local) | نجراني | Indigenous variety, medium-sized, amber |
| Barni Najran | برني نجراني | Large, semi-dry, prized locally |
| Sukkari | سكري | Sweet, crisp at Khalal stage (also grown in Qassim) |
| Safawi | صفاوي | Dark, soft, cylindrical |
| Ajwa | عجوة | Dark, soft, religiously significant |
| Khudri | خضري | Dark brown, moderately sweet, high yield |
| Mabroom | مبروم | Elongated, chewy, premium export |

Najran farmers practice traditional flood-basin irrigation supplemented by modern drip systems. The Saudi government's agricultural development programs have provided subsidized drip irrigation equipment, which has increased water use efficiency by 40-60%, consistent with the UNDP SIERY project findings in neighboring Hadhramaut.

### Q4: How is traditional hand pollination of date palms performed in Yemen and southern Saudi Arabia, and what are the key timing considerations?

**EN:** Date palms are dioecious -- male and female flowers grow on separate trees -- so artificial pollination is essential for fruit production. In Yemen (Hadhramaut) and Najran, traditional hand pollination (known locally as "ta'beer" / التأبير) has been practiced for millennia with remarkably consistent methods.

**Pollination Process:**

| Step | Procedure | Timing |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Pollen harvest | Cut mature male spadix (tal'a / طلعة) when it cracks open naturally | January-February |
| 2. Pollen drying | Dry male flowers in shade for 1-2 days; store in cloth or paper bags | Can be stored up to 1 year if refrigerated at 5-10 C |
| 3. Female readiness | Monitor female spadix; pollinate within 2-3 days of sheath (spaath) splitting | February-March (varies by variety and altitude) |
| 4. Insertion | Place 3-5 male flower strands (shamrookh / شمروخ) into each female spathe | Early morning (6-9 AM) for best pollen viability |
| 5. Tying | Tie the spathe loosely with palm fiber to hold pollen strands in place | Immediately after insertion |
| 6. Repeat | Each tree may have 8-15 spathes; pollinate each as it opens over 2-4 weeks | Staggered over the flowering season |

**Male-to-Female Ratio:**
- Recommended: 1 male tree per 25-50 female trees
- Minimum: 1 male per 100 females
- A strong male tree produces enough pollen for 50+ females

**Modern Alternatives Emerging:**
- Pollen suspension spray (water + pollen + starch) applied via pump sprayer
- Mechanical dusting from elevated platforms
- Both reduce labor time by 60-70% but traditional insertion still dominates in Hadhramaut

**Critical Factors:**
- Pollination within 2-3 days of spathe opening is essential; delay causes poor fruit set
- Wind, rain, or excessive humidity during pollination reduce success rates
- The quality of the male parent (fahl / فحل) affects fruit size and quality (metaxenia effect)

**AR:** نخيل التمر ثنائي المسكن -- الأزهار المذكرة والمؤنثة تنمو على أشجار منفصلة -- لذا فإن التلقيح الصناعي ضروري لإنتاج الثمار. في اليمن (حضرموت) ونجران، يُمارس التلقيح اليدوي التقليدي (المعروف محلياً بـ "التأبير") منذ آلاف السنين بطرق متسقة بشكل ملحوظ.

**خطوات التلقيح:**

| الخطوة | الإجراء | التوقيت |
|---|---|---|
| 1. جمع حبوب اللقاح | قطع الطلعة الذكرية الناضجة عند انشقاقها طبيعياً | يناير-فبراير |
| 2. تجفيف اللقاح | تجفيف الأزهار الذكرية في الظل 1-2 يوم؛ تخزين في أكياس قماش أو ورق | يمكن التخزين حتى سنة مع التبريد عند 5-10°م |
| 3. جاهزية الأنثى | مراقبة الطلعة الأنثوية؛ التلقيح خلال 2-3 أيام من انشقاق الغلاف | فبراير-مارس (يختلف حسب الصنف والارتفاع) |
| 4. الإدخال | وضع 3-5 شماريخ ذكر في كل طلعة أنثى | الصباح الباكر (6-9 صباحاً) لأفضل حيوية للقاح |
| 5. الربط | ربط الطلعة بليف النخل لتثبيت شماريخ اللقاح | فوراً بعد الإدخال |
| 6. التكرار | كل شجرة قد تحمل 8-15 طلعة؛ تلقيح كل واحدة عند انفتاحها خلال 2-4 أسابيع | موزع على موسم الإزهار |

**نسبة الذكور للإناث:**
- الموصى به: ذكر واحد لكل 25-50 أنثى
- الحد الأدنى: ذكر واحد لكل 100 أنثى
- شجرة ذكر قوية تنتج لقاحاً كافياً لأكثر من 50 أنثى

**البدائل الحديثة الناشئة:**
- رش معلق اللقاح (ماء + لقاح + نشا) بمضخة رش
- التعفير الآلي من منصات مرتفعة
- كلاهما يقلل وقت العمل بنسبة 60-70% لكن الإدخال التقليدي لا يزال سائداً في حضرموت

**عوامل حاسمة:**
- التلقيح خلال 2-3 أيام من انفتاح الطلعة ضروري؛ التأخير يسبب عقداً ضعيفاً
- الرياح والأمطار أو الرطوبة المفرطة أثناء التلقيح تقلل معدلات النجاح
- جودة الفحل (الأب المذكر) تؤثر على حجم الثمار وجودتها (تأثير الميتازينيا)

### Q5: What is the Red Palm Weevil (RPW) threat to date palms in Yemen and Najran, and how is it managed?

**EN:** The Red Palm Weevil (*Rhynchophorus ferrugineus* / سوسة النخيل الحمراء) is the most devastating pest of date palms in the Arabian Peninsula. It is classified as a lethal pest -- a single untreated infestation will kill the tree. RPW has been detected across Yemen (including Hadhramaut) and is a critical threat in Najran and all Saudi date-growing regions.

**RPW Life Cycle:**

| Stage | Duration | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Egg | 2-5 days | Laid in wounds or soft tissue on trunk, 200-300 eggs per female |
| Larva | 36-78 days | **The destructive stage** -- feeds inside the trunk, up to 5 cm long |
| Pupa | 14-21 days | Cocoon inside the trunk made of palm fiber |
| Adult | 2-3 months lifespan | 3-5 cm reddish-brown beetle; can fly 1-7 km |

**Symptoms by Stage:**

| Detection Stage | Symptoms |
|---|---|
| **Early** (difficult to detect) | Gnawing sounds inside trunk; small holes with frass (sawdust); fermentation odor |
| **Advanced** | Wilting of inner fronds; crown drooping or collapse; larvae visible in trunk; tree death |

**Action Threshold:** Any detection = immediate intervention. There is no tolerance threshold for RPW.

**Integrated Management Program:**

| Method | Details |
|---|---|
| **Pheromone traps** | 1 trap per 100 palms; replace pheromone every 6 weeks; inspect weekly |
| **Trunk injection** | Imidacloprid 20% or Emamectin benzoate 5% injected into 4-6 points at 15-20 cm depth, 45-degree angle |
| **Biological control** | *Beauveria bassiana* (entomopathogenic fungus) -- good efficacy |
| **Preventive spraying** | Chlorpyrifos on trunk monthly in high-risk areas |
| **Surgery** | Remove infested tissue, disinfect, seal with tar + insecticide (early infections only) |
| **Quarantine** | Never transport offshoots from infested areas; inspect all incoming planting material |
| **Sanitation** | Cover pruning wounds with tar; remove and burn dead palms |

**Economic Impact:** A mature date palm (15-40 years old) producing 80-150 kg/year at market value of 5,000-15,000 SAR per tree is permanently lost to a late-detected infestation.

**AR:** سوسة النخيل الحمراء (*Rhynchophorus ferrugineus*) هي أخطر آفة تصيب نخيل التمر في شبه الجزيرة العربية. تُصنف كآفة قاتلة -- إصابة واحدة غير معالجة ستقتل الشجرة. رُصدت السوسة في جميع أنحاء اليمن (بما في ذلك حضرموت) وتشكل تهديداً خطيراً في نجران وجميع مناطق التمور السعودية.

**دورة حياة سوسة النخيل الحمراء:**

| المرحلة | المدة | الوصف |
|---|---|---|
| بيضة | 2-5 أيام | تُوضع في الجروح أو الأنسجة اللينة على الجذع، 200-300 بيضة لكل أنثى |
| يرقة | 36-78 يوم | **المرحلة المدمرة** -- تتغذى داخل الجذع، طولها يصل إلى 5 سم |
| عذراء | 14-21 يوم | شرنقة داخل الجذع مصنوعة من ليف النخل |
| بالغ | 2-3 أشهر عمراً | خنفساء حمراء بنية 3-5 سم؛ يمكنها الطيران 1-7 كم |

**الأعراض حسب المرحلة:**

| مرحلة الكشف | الأعراض |
|---|---|
| **مبكرة** (صعبة الكشف) | صوت قرض داخل الجذع؛ ثقوب صغيرة مع نشارة؛ رائحة تخمر |
| **متقدمة** | ذبول السعف الداخلي؛ تدلي أو سقوط القمة النامية؛ يرقات مرئية في الجذع؛ موت الشجرة |

**عتبة التدخل:** أي كشف = تدخل فوري. لا يوجد حد تسامح لسوسة النخيل الحمراء.

**برنامج المكافحة المتكاملة:**

| الطريقة | التفاصيل |
|---|---|
| **المصائد الفرمونية** | مصيدة لكل 100 نخلة؛ تغيير الفرمون كل 6 أسابيع؛ فحص أسبوعي |
| **حقن الجذع** | Imidacloprid 20% أو Emamectin benzoate 5% حقن في 4-6 نقاط بعمق 15-20 سم بزاوية 45 درجة |
| **مكافحة حيوية** | *Beauveria bassiana* (فطر ممرض للحشرات) -- فعالية جيدة |
| **الرش الوقائي** | Chlorpyrifos على الجذع شهرياً في المناطق عالية الخطورة |
| **الجراحة** | إزالة الأنسجة المصابة، تعقيم، تغطية بقطران + مبيد (للإصابات المبكرة فقط) |
| **الحجر الزراعي** | عدم نقل فسائل من مناطق موبوءة أبداً؛ فحص جميع مواد الزراعة الواردة |
| **النظافة** | تغطية جروح التقليم بالقطران؛ إزالة وحرق النخيل الميت |

**الأثر الاقتصادي:** نخلة ناضجة (15-40 سنة) تنتج 80-150 كجم/سنة بقيمة سوقية 5,000-15,000 ريال سعودي للشجرة تُفقد نهائياً عند اكتشاف الإصابة متأخراً.

### Q6: What are the irrigation and fertilization requirements for date palms in the arid conditions of Hadhramaut and Najran?

**EN:** Date palms in Hadhramaut and Najran face extreme aridity (50-200 mm annual rainfall in Hadhramaut; 80-150 mm in Najran) with daily ET0 reaching 5.0-8.0 mm/day in summer. Proper irrigation and fertilization are critical for survival and production.

**Irrigation Requirements by Tree Age:**

| Tree Age | Annual Water Need (liters/tree) | Summer Frequency | Winter Frequency |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1-3 years (establishment) | 15,000-20,000 | Every 3-5 days | Every 14-20 days |
| 4-7 years (juvenile) | 25,000-35,000 | Every 3-5 days | Every 14-20 days |
| 8+ years (production) | 40,000-60,000 | Every 3-5 days (200-300 L/tree) | Every 14-20 days (100-150 L/tree) |

**Crop Coefficient (Kc) by Growth Stage** (from Yemen SAHOOL data):

| Stage | Period | Kc Value |
|---|---|---|
| Dormancy | Nov-Jan | 0.80 |
| Pollination | Feb-Mar | 0.90 |
| Fruit development | Mar-Aug | 1.00 |
| Ripening (Rutab) | Aug-Sep | 0.85 |
| Harvest (Tamr) | Sep-Nov | 0.75 |

**Irrigation Systems Comparison:**

| System | Water Efficiency | Suitability | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Drip** (recommended) | 90%+ | Best for water-scarce regions | 4-8 emitters/tree, 8-16 L/hr; UNDP SIERY trials showed 40-60% water savings in Hadhramaut |
| **Flood/Basin** (traditional) | 50-60% | Still common in Hadhramaut wadis | Every 7-14 days; wastes water but culturally entrenched |
| **Bubbler** | 80-85% | Good alternative | 1-2 bubblers per tree basin |

### Q9: What are the planting, harvesting, and oil extraction practices for sesame in Tihama?

**EN:** Sesame cultivation in Tihama follows a well-established seasonal cycle driven by the summer monsoon rains. The entire process from land preparation to oil extraction involves traditional techniques refined over generations.

**Planting Calendar and Field Preparation:**

| Activity | Timing | Details |
|---|---|---|
| Land preparation | May-June | Plow 2-3 times; level field for spate irrigation channels |
| Seed bed | Early June | Fine, firm seedbed; soil temperature must exceed 20 C for germination |
| Planting | June-July | Broadcast or drill at 3-5 kg seed/hectare; row spacing 50-60 cm |
| Thinning | 15-20 days after emergence | Thin to 10-15 cm between plants within rows |

**Irrigation Schedule (for irrigated sesame):**

| Growth Stage | Frequency | Water Amount | Kc Value |
|---|---|---|---|
| Germination | Every 5-7 days | 15-20 mm | 0.35 |
| Vegetative growth | Every 7-10 days | 25-30 mm | 0.70 |
| Flowering and fruit set (critical) | Every 5-7 days | 30-40 mm | 1.05 |
| Capsule formation | Every 7-10 days | 25-30 mm | 0.80 |
| Maturity | No irrigation | -- | 0.25 |

**Fertilization:**

| Timing | Fertilizer | Rate (kg/ha) |
|---|---|---|
| Before planting (basal) | Superphosphate | 100-150 |
| Before planting (basal) | Potassium sulfate | 50-80 |
| Before planting (basal) | Urea (starter) | 30-40 |
| 25-30 days after planting | Urea (46% N) top dress | 50-60 |
| Start of flowering | Potassium sulfate top dress | 40 |

**Harvest Indicators and Method:**

| Indicator | Description |
|---|---|
| Lower leaves yellowing and falling | First sign of maturity |
| Lower capsules turning brown | Ready for harvest |
| Lower capsules beginning to split | Harvest immediately (shattering risk) |
| Days to harvest | 85-120 days from planting depending on variety |

**Traditional Harvest Method (Tihama):**
1. Cut plants at base when lower capsules turn brown but before they open
2. Bundle plants upright in sheaves (huzam / حُزم) of 15-20 stalks
3. Stand sheaves in field for 7-14 days to dry (the capsules open naturally)
4. Invert sheaves and shake/beat over clean cloth or tarpaulin to collect seeds
5. Winnow to remove chaff and debris

**Sesame Oil Extraction (zeit al-saleet / زيت السليط):**

| Method | Description | Oil Recovery | Quality |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Traditional stone press** (ma'sarah / معصرة) | Seeds roasted lightly, ground in stone mill, pressed by hand or animal-powered lever | 35-40% | Excellent flavor, dark golden |
| **Mechanical screw press** | Seeds fed into modern expeller press | 40-45% | Good, faster processing |
| **Solvent extraction** | Industrial hexane extraction | 48-52% | Maximum yield but requires refining |
| **Cold press** | No roasting, hydraulic press | 38-42% | Lightest color, mildest flavor, highest nutrition |

**Sesame Products:**

| Product (EN) | المنتج (AR) | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Zeit al-Saleet | زيت السليط | Traditional sesame oil -- cooking, medicine, cosmetics |
| Tahini | طحينة | Ground sesame paste for hummus, sauces, and desserts |
| Halawah | حلاوة طحينية | Confection of tahini + sugar/honey |
| Sesame seeds (whole) | بذور سمسم | Topping for bread, pastries, and savory dishes |
| Sesame cake (residue) | كسبة السمسم | High-protein animal feed (35-40% protein) after oil extraction |

### Q4: How is traditional hand pollination of date palms performed, and what is the recommended male-to-female ratio?
**EN:** Date palms are dioecious (separate male and female trees), so artificial pollination is essential for commercial production. Traditional hand pollination remains the dominant method in Yemen and southern Saudi Arabia.

**Step-by-step process:**

| Step | Action | Details |
|------|--------|---------|
| 1 | **Pollen collection** | Harvest male spathes (طلع ذكر) when they split open naturally, typically February-March |
| 2 | **Pollen storage** | Dry pollen at room temperature for 24-48 hours; store in airtight containers at 5-10 C for up to 1 year |
| 3 | **Timing** | Pollinate within 2-3 days after the female spathe (طلعة أنثى) splits open; morning hours are optimal |
| 4 | **Insertion method** | Place 3-5 male flower strands (شماريخ ذكرية) inside each female spathe and tie loosely |
| 5 | **Spray method** | Alternatively, mix pollen with water or talcum powder and spray onto female flowers using a hand pump |
| 6 | **Repeat** | Each female tree may produce 10-20 spathes over 2-3 weeks; each must be pollinated individually |
| 7 | **Verification** | Check fruit set 2-3 weeks after pollination; re-pollinate any unfertilized spathes |

**Male-to-female tree ratio:**
- Recommended: **1 male per 25-50 female trees**
- Minimum viable: **1 male per 100 female trees**
- Pollen from one healthy male can pollinate 25-50 females per season

In Hadhramaut, skilled climbers (called "naqqa" or "tayyab") ascend palms using a traditional belt (حبل التسلق) to perform pollination, a skill passed from father to son. Modern mechanical pollination devices are being introduced but have limited adoption in Yemen due to cost and terrain.

**AR:** النخيل ثنائي المسكن (أشجار ذكر وأنثى منفصلة)، لذا فإن التلقيح الاصطناعي ضروري للإنتاج التجاري. يبقى التلقيح اليدوي التقليدي هو الطريقة السائدة في اليمن وجنوب السعودية.

**خطوات التلقيح:**

| الخطوة | الإجراء | التفاصيل |
|--------|---------|----------|
| 1 | **جمع حبوب اللقاح** | حصاد طلع الذكور عند انشقاقه طبيعياً، عادةً فبراير-مارس |
| 2 | **تخزين اللقاح** | تجفيف اللقاح في درجة حرارة الغرفة 24-48 ساعة؛ تخزين في حاويات محكمة عند 5-10 م لمدة تصل إلى سنة |
| 3 | **التوقيت** | التلقيح خلال 2-3 أيام من انشقاق الطلعة الأنثوية؛ الصباح الباكر هو الأفضل |
| 4 | **طريقة الإدخال** | وضع 3-5 شماريخ ذكرية داخل كل طلعة أنثى وربطها بشكل فضفاض |
| 5 | **طريقة الرش** | بديلاً، خلط اللقاح مع الماء أو بودرة التلك ورشه على الأزهار الأنثوية بمضخة يدوية |
| 6 | **التكرار** | كل شجرة أنثى قد تنتج 10-20 طلعة على مدى 2-3 أسابيع؛ كل طلعة تُلقّح فردياً |

**نسبة الذكور إلى الإناث:**

### Q5: How dangerous is the Red Palm Weevil (RPW) and what are the detection and control strategies?
**EN:** The Red Palm Weevil (*Rhynchophorus ferrugineus*, سوسة النخيل الحمراء) is classified as the most lethal pest of date palms. A single infestation can kill a mature palm worth 15,000+ SAR. The SAHOOL platform treats any RPW detection as a **critical alert requiring response within 24-48 hours**.

**Life cycle and damage:**

| Stage | Duration | Details |
|-------|----------|---------|
| Egg | 2-5 days | Female lays 200-300 eggs in wounds/crevices |
| Larva | 36-78 days | **Destructive stage** -- burrows inside trunk, feeding on soft tissue |
| Pupa | 14-21 days | Pupates inside cocoon within the trunk |
| Adult | 2-3 months | 3-5 cm long, reddish-brown, can fly 1+ km |

**Early detection symptoms (difficult):**
- Sound of gnawing inside the trunk
- Small holes with frass (sawdust-like excrement)
- Fermentation odor from trunk wounds
- Wilting of inner (youngest) fronds

**Advanced symptoms (tree often lost):**
- Collapse of crown (toppling of growing point)
- Visible larvae inside trunk cavities
- Complete trunk hollowing
- Tree death

**Integrated control strategy:**

| Method | Details | Priority |
|--------|---------|----------|
| **Pheromone traps** | 1 trap per 100 palms; replace pheromone every 6 weeks; inspect weekly | Essential |
| **Monthly inspection** | Physical examination of all palms, especially trunk base and crown | Essential |
| **Trunk injection** | Imidacloprid 20% or Emamectin benzoate 5% at 4-6 points, 15-20 cm deep at 45 angle | Early infection |
| **Trunk spray** | Chlorpyrifos on trunk exterior (21-day safety period) | Supplementary |
| **Biological control** | *Beauveria bassiana* (entomopathogenic fungus) | Good efficacy |
| **Surgical removal** | Remove infected tissue, sterilize, seal with tar and insecticide | Early infection |
| **Quarantine** | Never transport offshoots from infested areas | Prevention |
| **Wound treatment** | Cover all pruning wounds with tar immediately | Prevention |

**Action threshold:** Any detection = immediate intervention. There is no population threshold -- a single weevil sighting triggers the full response protocol.

**AR:** تُصنّف سوسة النخيل الحمراء (*Rhynchophorus ferrugineus*) كأخطر آفة قاتلة لنخيل التمر. إصابة واحدة قد تقتل نخلة ناضجة قيمتها أكثر من 15,000 ريال. تُعامل منصة سهول أي كشف لسوسة النخيل كـ**تنبيه حرج يتطلب استجابة خلال 24-48 ساعة**.

**دورة الحياة والضرر:**

| المرحلة | المدة | التفاصيل |

### Q9: What is the complete sesame cultivation cycle in Tihama -- from planting through harvest to oil extraction?
**EN:** Sesame cultivation in Tihama follows a well-defined seasonal cycle tied to monsoon rains and wadi spate flows. The entire cycle spans approximately 110 days.

**Planting (June-July):**

| Parameter | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| Soil preparation | Light plowing after first rains; level fields for spate water distribution |
| Seed rate | 3-5 kg/ha (broadcast) or 2-3 kg/ha (row planting) |
| Row spacing | 40-60 cm between rows, 10-15 cm between plants |
| Soil temperature | Minimum 20 C for germination (optimum 25-30 C) |
| Planting method | Broadcast after flood recession or drill-seeded in rows |

**Growth stages and management:**

| Stage | Days | Kc | Key Management Actions |
|-------|------|-----|----------------------|
| Germination/Initial | 0-20 | 0.35 | Ensure soil moisture; thin seedlings to 10-15 cm spacing |
| Vegetative growth | 20-50 | 0.70 | First weeding at 20-25 days; top-dress with 50-60 kg/ha Urea |
| Flowering/Fruiting (critical) | 50-90 | 1.05 | Irrigation if available every 5-7 days; apply 40 kg/ha K2SO4; monitor for aphids and capsule borers |
| Maturity | 90-110 | 0.25 | Stop irrigation; lower leaves yellow and drop; lower capsules turn brown |

**Common pests and diseases:**

| Problem | Arabic | Control |
|---------|--------|---------|
| Fusarium wilt | ذبول الفيوزاريوم | Resistant varieties, crop rotation (3-4 year cycle) |
| Charcoal rot | عفن الفحم | Avoid water stress during flowering |
| Sesame aphid | من السمسم | Neem oil 1-2%, or Imidacloprid if severe |
| Capsule borer | دودة كبسولات السمسم | Early harvest; Emamectin benzoate spray |
| Green leafhopper | الجاسيد الأخضر | Neem-based sprays |

**Harvest (October-November):**
1. Cut plants when lower capsules begin to split and leaves yellow (85-120 days after planting)
2. Bundle plants upright in sheaves (حُزم) and lean against supports for sun-drying (7-14 days)
3. When capsules are fully dry, invert sheaves over a mat or tarp and shake/beat to release seeds
4. Winnow seeds to remove chaff and debris

**Expected yields:**

| System | Yield (t/ha) |
|--------|-------------|
| Traditional rainfed | 0.3-0.6 |
| Improved rainfed | 0.6-1.0 |
| Irrigated | 1.0-1.5 |
| Intensive irrigated | 1.5-2.0 |

**Traditional oil extraction (Zayt al-Saleet / زيت السليط):**
1. Clean and dry seeds thoroughly (moisture below 6%)
2. Roast seeds lightly in a flat pan (optional -- enhances flavor)
3. Grind roasted seeds in a stone or mechanical press
4. Press ground paste in a traditional screw press (معصرة) or hydraulic press
5. Collect oil and allow to settle for 2-3 days
6. Decant clear oil; filter if needed
7. Oil yield: approximately 45-55% of seed weight
8. Store in dark glass or tin containers; shelf life 6-12 months unrefrigerated

### Q10: What are the key differences and complementarities between date palm and sesame cultivation in Yemen's agricultural system?
**EN:** Date palms and sesame occupy distinct ecological niches within Yemen's farming landscape but together form a complementary agricultural system:

| Aspect | Date Palm (النخيل) | Sesame (السمسم) |
|--------|-------------------|-----------------|
| **Type** | Perennial tree (60-80 year lifespan) | Annual summer crop (110 days) |
| **Primary region** | Hadhramaut, eastern plateau | Tihama coastal plain |
| **Water need** | 40,000-60,000 L/tree/year (high) | 300-500 mm/season (low) |
| **Salinity tolerance** | Up to 4-12 dS/m (excellent) | Up to 2.5 dS/m (moderate) |
| **Temperature range** | 20-45 C optimal, survives 52 C | 25-40 C optimal, no frost tolerance |
| **Investment** | High upfront (first fruit at 4-5 years) | Low input, single-season return |
| **Revenue** | Steady annual income from year 5-80 | Seasonal cash income |
| **Labor** | Year-round (pruning, pollination, harvest) | Seasonal (3-4 months) |
| **Primary products** | Dates (fresh/dried), syrup, paste | Oil (Zayt al-Saleet), tahini, seeds |
| **Food security role** | Staple calorie source (277 kcal/100g) | Essential cooking oil and protein |
| **Cultural significance** | Sacred tree, identity symbol | Traditional oil crop heritage |
| **Climate risk** | RPW pest, groundwater depletion | Drought stress, Fusarium wilt |
| **SAHOOL crop Kc (mid)** | 1.00 | 1.05 |

**Complementary roles in the farming system:**
1. **Risk diversification**: Perennial date income buffers against sesame crop failure, and vice versa
2. **Seasonal labor**: Sesame planting/harvest (June-November) overlaps minimally with date pollination (February-March) and date harvest (August-October)
3. **Nutritional completeness**: Dates provide carbohydrates and minerals; sesame provides fats and protein
4. **Market diversity**: Dates serve local, regional, and diaspora markets; sesame oil serves the domestic cooking oil market
5. **Water efficiency**: Sesame's low water requirement allows cultivation in areas where date palm irrigation is not feasible
